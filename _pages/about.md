About 
======


<div style="text-align: justify;">

I am an AI researcher working on sociotechnical alignment of high-risk AI systems. My background is in methodology, statistics, and data science. My current work focuses on AI systems used for clinical decision support. I hold appointments at <a href="https://www.uu.nl/staff/DAnadria" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="inline-link">Utrecht University</a> (dept. Methodology & Statistics) with affiliate appointments at University Medical Center Utrecht (Julius Center) and <a href="https://www.tudelft.nl/en/staff/d.anadria/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="inline-link">Delft University of Technology</a>  (fac. Technology Policy and Management). I leverage systems thinking and computational skills to create frameworks for responsible AI system development, implementation, and monitoring. My outputs strive to pave the way for competitive, compliant, and safe AI systems that can safeguard the values of human dignity, freedom, democracy, equality, the rule of law, and human rights in high-risk domains.

</div>
